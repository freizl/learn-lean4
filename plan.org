* Goal

Become comfortable with theorem proving in Lean 4, understand how formal verification works in practice (e.g., AWS Cedar), and build foundation for deeper PL/type theory knowledge.

** Commitment
- Duration: 3 months (Jan 27 - Apr 27, 2026)
- Time: 4-6 hours/week
- Rule: No switching to Coq or other tools. Stay focused.

** Why Lean 4
- Modern, clean design
- Functional programming + theorem proving
- Real-world usage: AWS Cedar (authorization policy verification)
- Good tooling (VS Code, LSP)

* Rules for Staying Focused

1. *No switching tools* - Lean 4 only for 3 months
2. *No "research" rabbit holes* - If you see an interesting Coq paper, ignore it
3. *Consistency over intensity* - 4 hrs/week for 12 weeks beats 20 hrs for 2 weeks then nothing
4. *When stuck, ask for help* - Lean Zulip, or Claude
5. *Track progress* - Check off boxes above
