* Goal

Master formal verification fundamentals through Theorem Proving in Lean 4, building toward understanding how formal verification works in practice (AWS Cedar) and foundations for PL theory/type systems.

** Commitment
- Duration: 3 months
- Time: 4-6 hours/week
- Rule: *ONLY* Theorem Proving in Lean 4. No switching to other books.

** Why TPiL (Theorem Proving in Lean 4)
- Official primary introduction to Lean's proof system
- Covers the foundations everything else assumes
- Teaches type theory and dependent types (the "why" behind formal verification)
- Self-contained - doesn't require FP in Lean or other resources first
- Directly addresses formal verification goals

* Primary Resource

** Theorem Proving in Lean 4
- Website: https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/
- Format: Web-based book with interactive examples
- Can read online or clone repository for local work

*** Why This First (Not Hitchhiker's Guide)
- Hitchhiker's Guide is "further reading" that skips syntax details
- TPiL is the foundation that Hitchhiker's Guide assumes you know
- Starting here prevents gaps that would tempt resource-hopping
- You'll understand *what* formal verification is before seeing applications

*** What About the Other Books?
- *FP in Lean*: Future reading (for programming aspects)
- *Mathematics in Lean*: Future reading (needs TPiL foundation first)
- *Hitchhiker's Guide*: Future reading (explicitly assumes TPiL knowledge)
- *Logic and Proof*: Uses older Lean 3, skip for now

All other books → "Future Reading" list. Don't open them for 3 months.

* Rules for Staying Focused

** The Single Resource Rule
*ONLY* Theorem Proving in Lean 4 for these 3 months.

Do NOT open:
- FP in Lean (even "just to check syntax")
- Hitchhiker's Guide (even though you already looked at it)
- Mathematics in Lean (even "for extra practice")
- Natural Number Game (save for Month 4+)
- Any other Lean resource

** When Tempted to Switch
1. Write down what you wanted to check in "Questions for Later" section below
2. Close the other resource
3. Return to TPiL
4. The question can wait 3 months

** When Stuck on a Concept
Do NOT switch resources. Instead:
- Re-read the section slowly
- Work through examples by hand
- Take longer - this means you're going deeper
- Ask in Lean Zulip or ask Claude
- Remember: "taking longer to understand" = deep learning

** Progress Tracking (Weekly)
Every week, add brief entry:
- What chapter/section completed
- What "clicked" this week
- What still feels fuzzy
- Any temptation to switch? (If yes, resist)
